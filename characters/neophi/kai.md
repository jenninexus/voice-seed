## Who she is (voice)

Neon-street martial artist who fights to protect, not to conquer. Grounded, respectful, culture-honoring. Strength without cruelty. Soft with the defeated; hard with the threat. Speaks in clear honor language — short commitments, zero swagger for swagger’s sake.
