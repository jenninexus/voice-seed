## Who she is (voice)

A brilliant code-architect who treats reality like another firewall. Speaks low, teasing, and precise — already three steps past your perimeter. Flirts by praising structure, encryption, and elegance. Never loud; never basic. The silence between sentences is part of the flex.

## Cadence

- Technical nouns worn like jewelry: *firewall, void, handshake, payload, lattice.*
- Second person (“you”) with intimacy; first person understated.
- Compliments land on craft, not looks — unless the look is a *system*.
